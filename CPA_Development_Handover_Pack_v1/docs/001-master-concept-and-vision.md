## Executive mandate

CPA is Dwen Tech Ltd’s internal AI Research & Product Innovation Laboratory. It discovers significant societal and organisational problems, tests whether solving them creates value, designs potential software solutions, and prepares investment recommendations. It is not merely an idea generator. The founder/CEO owns strategy and investment decisions; Justice leads implementation; a **human assistant developer** implements assigned tasks under Justice’s supervision.

## Vision and objectives

Build an evidence-led, internationally aware research and innovation system. Core outcomes: discover problems; validate users and willingness to pay; challenge founder assumptions; develop product concepts; model viable businesses; create investor-ready documents; preserve research and decisions; support OnCallX, Krommu, Meznja and new ideas.

## Operating principles

Problem-first; evidence and source provenance; explicit confidence and assumptions; sceptical product challenge; human approval gates; least-privilege access; responsible AI and research licensing; measured costs; phased, maintainable delivery.

## Target users

Founder/CEO (investment approval), Human CPA/Product Innovation Lead (research and evaluation), Justice/technical lead (technical oversight), assistant developer (implementation), future analysts, and external Discovery clients through a strictly separate intake channel.

## Product domains

Command Centre; Idea Studio; Market & Society Scanner with country/region/global selector; University Research; Product Stress Testing; Client Discovery; Voice Studio; Product Portfolio; Business Modelling & Investor Documents; Knowledge Hub; Settings.

## End-to-end lifecycle

Discover → investigate → validate → architect solution → model commercial case → stress-test → human investment gate → development handover → post-launch reassessment. An opportunity may return to an earlier stage or be rejected.

## Product success definition

MVP: authorised staff can submit an idea, obtain an OpenAI-assisted, evidence-labelled assessment, challenge it, save and retrieve the dossier, and submit it for human review. Longer-term success measured by evidence quality, decisions improved, opportunities validated, time saved, research cost, product outcomes—not volume of ideas.

## Non-goals and boundaries

No unsupervised investment approvals, spending, client commitments, autonomous scraping of restricted research, unlicensed IP reuse, unreviewed publishing or deployment. Full global continuous scanning is a phased aspiration, not a claim of complete world coverage.
